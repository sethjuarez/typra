test(emitter): extend coerce-union dispatch fixture to all three prompty seams

Broaden the `dispatch-union-coerce` fixture from the Format seam alone to all
three prompty seam shapes, proving the coerce-aware path resolution end to end
against prompty's real schema shapes:

- Renderer  (CLOSED union): discriminator field IS the coerce target
  (`kind` == coerce `#{ kind }`) -> `agent.template.format.kind`.
- Executor  (OPEN union + `*` wildcard): the load-bearing case where the coerce
  target DIFFERS from the discriminator. `@coerce(Model, string, #{ id })`
  targets `id`, but dispatch resolves `provider`; the bare-string shorthand
  yields `{ id }` with NO provider yet emit still succeeds and
  `agent.model.provider` resolves through the coerce-canonical arm. The open
  `string` union member makes the decl `isClosed: false`; the `CustomModel
  { provider: "*" }` catch-all lowers to the fallback `defaultVariant` (the
  downstream-registry delegation hook) while the known literal providers stay
  enumerated `variants`.
- Parser    (CLOSED union): a second field-IS-coerce-target seam on a distinct
  model -> `agent.parser.kind`.

This confirms the fix composes with (a) the id-vs-provider coerce/discriminator
mismatch and (b) open discriminator unions with a wildcard arm, not just closed
unions. Also registers the fixture in the validate-fixtures allow-list.
